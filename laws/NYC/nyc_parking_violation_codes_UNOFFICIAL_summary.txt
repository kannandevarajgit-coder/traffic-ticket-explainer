New York City parking, standing and camera violation fines
UNOFFICIAL SUMMARY. Restated from the official page below on 2026-11-22, not copied word for word. Replace or supplement with the official document and verify at the source before relying on it.
Source: https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/services-violation-codes.page
NYC parking and camera tickets are handled by the NYC Department of Finance. They carry fines but no license points. Driving (moving) violations in NYC follow New York State law and its point system.

Notes from the page
- Each fine includes a 15 dollar New York State Criminal Justice surcharge.
- Code 27 (disabled-parking zone, off-street) includes a 30 dollar surcharge.
- Codes 5, 12, 15 and 43 (bus lane, MTA bus lane, MTA double parking, MTA bus stop) have fines that rise with each repeat violation within 12 months.
- Some codes are no longer issued after the August 1, 2018 traffic rule revisions.
- Parking violation rules are in Title 19, Chapter 39 of the Rules of the City of New York. Traffic and alternate side parking rules are set by NYC DOT.
