How points work in New York
- Points are added only after a conviction. They are counted from the date of the violation, not the date of conviction.
- Only points from violations in the last 24 months count toward the total.
- Points stay on the driving record as long as the conviction does, and insurance companies may use them.
- 11 or more points in 24 months may lead to suspension of the driver license.
- 6 or more points in 18 months requires paying a Driver Responsibility Assessment fee.
- Out-of-state convictions generally do not add New York points, except convictions in Canada (reciprocal agreement).
- No points for bicycle, pedestrian or parking violations, for unregistered, unlicensed or uninsured operation, for most equipment and inspection violations, or for business-related violations.
- Point and Insurance Reduction Program (PIRP) course: 4 points are subtracted when calculating a suspension for drivers with 11 or more points (points are not removed from the record), and liability and collision insurance premiums drop 10 percent.

Points by violation in New York
- Speeding 1 to 10 mph over the limit: 3 points
- Speeding 11 to 20 mph over: 4 points
- Speeding 21 to 30 mph over: 6 points
- Speeding 31 to 40 mph over: 8 points
- Speeding more than 40 mph over: 11 points
- Speeding, amount not specified: 3 points
- Speeding in a work zone: 8 points
- Alcohol or drug-related driving violation: 11 points
- Aggravated unlicensed operation: 11 points
- Reckless driving: 5 points
- Speed contest or race: 5 points
- Leaving the scene of an accident with personal injury: 5 points
- Failure to exercise due care: 5 points
- Using a mobile phone or portable electronic device while driving: 5 points
- Railroad crossing violation: 5 points
- Passing a stopped school bus: 8 points
- Over-height vehicle or bridge strike: 8 points
- Following too closely (tailgating): 4 points
- Improper passing, unsafe lane change, driving left of center, or driving in the wrong direction: 3 points
- Failing to obey a traffic signal, stop sign or yield sign: 3 points
- Failing to yield the right-of-way: 3 points
- Leaving the scene of an accident with property damage or injury to a domestic animal: 3 points
- Child under 16 not properly restrained: 3 points
- Any other moving violation: 2 points
