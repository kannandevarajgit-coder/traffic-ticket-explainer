New Jersey Motor Vehicle Points Schedule
UNOFFICIAL SUMMARY. Restated from the official page below on 2026-11-22, not copied word for word. Replace or supplement with the official document and verify at the source before relying on it.
Source: https://www.nj.gov/mvc/license/points-schedule.htm
The official page lists the NJSA section, the offense and the points. It does not state suspension thresholds or how long points count.

Points by offense in New Jersey (NJSA section in brackets)
- Speeding 1 to 14 mph over the limit [39:4-98]: 2 points
- Speeding 15 to 29 mph over [39:4-98]: 4 points
- Speeding 30 mph or more over [39:4-98]: 5 points
- Following too closely, tailgating [39:4-89]: 5 points
- Reckless driving [39:4-96]: 5 points
- Racing on a highway [39:4-52, 39:5C-1]: 5 points
- Improper passing of a school bus [39:4-128.1]: 5 points
- Failure to pass to the right of an oncoming vehicle [39:4-84]: 5 points
- Improper passing on the right or off the roadway [39:4-85]: 4 points
- Passing in a no-passing zone [39:4-86]: 4 points
- Improper passing of a frozen dessert truck [39:4-128.4]: 4 points
- Driving in an unsafe manner [39:4-97.2]: 4 points, only for a third or later offense within five years
- Handheld cellphone or electronic device [39:4-97.3]: 3 points, assessed for a third offense within 10 years of a second offense, and later offenses
- Improper turn at a traffic light [39:4-115]: 3 points
- Improper right or left turn [39:4-123]: 3 points
- Improper turn from an approved course [39:4-124]: 3 points
- Improper U-turn [39:4-125]: 3 points
- Red-light camera violation [39:4-81]: 0 points
- Leaving the scene of an accident, no personal injury [39:4-129]: 2 points
- Leaving the scene of an accident, personal injury [39:4-129]: 8 points
- Careless driving [39:4-97]: 2 points
- Failure to yield at an intersection [39:4-90]: 2 points
- Failure to obey a police officer directing traffic [39:4-80]: 2 points
- Failure to signal [39:4-126]: 2 points
- Failure to stop for a traffic light [39:4-105]: 2 points
- Failure to observe stop or yield signs [39:4-144]: 2 points
- Moving violation committed out of state [39:5D-4]: 2 points
- On the Turnpike, Garden State Parkway or Atlantic City Expressway: moving against traffic 2 points; improper passing 4 points; unlawful use of the median strip [27:23-29] 2 points
- Most other listed moving offenses: 2 points
